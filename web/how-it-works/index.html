02

The model reads the claim

On-device AI, through Firebase AI Logic, turns the wording into a structured claim. It does not issue Safe, Warning, or Neutral.

04

A stamp, then the verdict

Safe needs a sender the app verified (a sample thread or the extension; a pasted email stops at Neutral), nothing risky asked, and either a parcel you expect from that carrier's official domain or an order stored on this device from a vetted store's exact domain. Anything else is Warning or Neutral. Every AI result is labelled with where it ran.
