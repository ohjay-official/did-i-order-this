About

Built to check a parcel claim against what you ordered

Did I Order This? is a delivery-scam guard for email. It was built by Oh-Jay as an entry for #BuildHybridAI 2026, the Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference hackathon.

From DHL Express Desk <alerts@dhl-parcel-desk.example>

Your parcel is held. Pay the release fee.

A message like this can look official. Without a way to check the claim against purchases and tracking numbers that never leave your laptop, a fake parcel message looks like a real one.

The problem

A delivery, renewal, invoice, or refund email can ask you to pay a fee or click a link. The usual advice is to look carefully. This product compares the claim with what you actually ordered.

The approach

On-device AI reads what the email claims. Deterministic code checks that claim against the orders stored on the device. Cloud inference is a fallback on the hosted site, and only after you agree.
