FAQ

Questions we can answer honestly

If a question is not here, it is probably because the product does not do that yet.

Does my email leave this device?

Not by default. A check starts on the device. On the hosted site, the email goes to the cloud only if on-device AI is unavailable and you agree. The extension does not send mail to the cloud.

What does "Checked on this device" mean?

It is the label shown when Firebase AI Logic reports that the claim was read with on-device inference. "Checked in the cloud" means you agreed to a cloud read. "Limited check (no AI)" means code ran without a model. "Check not completed" means the AI read did not finish.

Why is the verdict not written by the model?

The model is asked to read messy wording into a structured claim. Code then matches that claim to your ledger, official domains, and known lure patterns. That keeps the Safe, Warning, and Neutral rules stable.

What files can I import?

An Amazon Retail.OrderHistory CSV or a ZIP that contains one, a PayPal activity CSV, or a generic CSV with common columns such as order id, tracking, merchant, date, and amount. The file stays in the browser. Jumia, Walmart, and Shopify customer exports are not promised.

Do I need the Chrome extension to try this?

No. Judges and anyone on a phone can use the hosted sample inbox. The extension is for checking an open Gmail thread on a laptop that can run on-device AI.

Does this work on iPhone or Android?

The hosted site can be opened on a phone, and you can look at the sample inbox and the verdicts. On-device Gemini Nano is a desktop Chrome capability. A phone browser will usually be offered a cloud check, which still requires your agreement, or a limited check with no AI.

Can it check SMS or WhatsApp lures?

No. Delivery texts, MMS, and chat apps are out of scope. So are account-locked or "log in to your account" emails.

Is there a paid plan?

Not today. The hosted checker and the extension, as they work now, are free to use. See the pricing page.
