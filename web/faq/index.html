Questions we can answer honestly

Privacy and where a check runs

Does my email leave this device?

Not by default. A check starts on the device. On the hosted site, the email goes to the cloud only if on-device AI is unavailable and you agree. The extension does not send mail to the cloud.

What does "Checked on this device" mean?

On-device inference through Firebase AI Logic produced the structured claim, and code on this device issued the verdict.

What does "Checked in the cloud" mean?

On-device AI was not available, you agreed in the consent dialog, and the hosted site sent the text of that one email for cloud inference. This never happens in the Chrome extension.

What does "Limited check (no AI)" mean?

No model ran. The product still compares obvious tracking-like numbers and the sender domain with the official-domain pack and with records stored on this device. It is not a full verdict.

What does "Check not completed" mean?

The model did not return a usable claim, or the check stopped before a verdict. On the landing hero, a sample thread can still show a prepared claim, with a note that on-device AI did not run in that browser.

Why is the verdict not written by the model?

The model is only asked to read what the email claims: the party, the domain, cited numbers, and whether it asks you to pay or click. Deterministic code compares that claim with orders and tracking numbers stored on this device.

How a verdict is decided

Why was a UPS email marked Warning?

Carrier mail is judged by the cited tracking number, not by how official the domain looks. If that number is not an expected parcel stored on this device, the check is Warning. In the default sample, there are no expected parcels.

What is the official-domain pack?

A public list of official carrier and store domains. It can add a reason to the verdict. It does not replace the tracking or order-number check.

What files can I import?

An Amazon, PayPal, or generic order-history CSV or ZIP. Files are parsed in the browser and are not uploaded.

Import, storage, and the inbox

Where are my orders stored?

In this browser for the hosted app, or in chrome.storage.local for the extension. They are not kept on the project server.

Does this scan my whole inbox?

No. The hosted app checks a pasted email or a sample thread. The extension reads only the Gmail thread that is open, as text.

Do I need the Chrome extension to try this?

No. The hosted sample inbox is enough. The extension is for checking a Gmail thread you already have open.

The extension and other limits

Is the extension in the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. You can sideload the unpacked build. A store listing is planned and is not available now.

Can I check a screenshot or a .eml file?

Not today. There is no screenshot or .eml ingest. You can paste the email text, open a sample thread, import an order-history file, or use the extension on an open Gmail thread.

What happens if I click Do not send?

The email stays on the device. You can run a limited check with no AI, or try again later if on-device AI becomes available.

Can I save a shipment after a check?

Yes, when the product offers it, for example after a store shipment notice. You confirm first. The Save this shipment control is in the sample inbox.

Does on-device AI work in every browser?

No. It depends on Firebase AI Logic and on-device support in that browser. If it does not run, the hosted site may offer a cloud check after consent, or a limited check with no AI.

Is there a paid plan?

No. There is no paid plan to buy today, and no subscription form on the pricing page.

Can I use this on my phone?

The hosted site works in a mobile browser, including the sample inbox. The Chrome extension is for desktop Chrome with Gmail.

Who built this, and is a company endorsing it?

It was built by Oh-Jay as an entry for #BuildHybridAI 2026, the Firebase AI Logic hybrid inference hackathon. No company has endorsed this product.
